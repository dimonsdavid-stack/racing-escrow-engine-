A

Two distinct currencies

Gold Coins (GC) are non-transferable social-play utility tokens. They carry no monetary value and cannot be redeemed for cash or prizes outside the platform.

Sweeps Coins (SC) are promotional entries issued through approved promotional allocations and free entry methods. SC won through valid, verified challenges may be eligible for monetary prizes at 1 SC = $1 USD, subject to the active program's eligibility and redemption rules. SC are never sold directly.

B

Eligibility & account integrity

The active program registry specifies minimum age, eligible territories and dates. Identity, age, current physical location and rules acceptance are checked before SC participation. Client-entered location or an unchecked customer ID does not approve access.

One natural person may hold one account. Multi-accounting, telemetry manipulation and identity or location masking can trigger review and suspension. Disputed balances are preserved for a documented review; automatic forfeiture is not asserted here.

The founder's proposed minimum was 18 or the local age of majority, with WA, ID, MI and NV exclusions. Those four exclusions are not a complete or verified territorial policy. The deployed engine uses an affirmative allowlist from the operator's actual active program.

C

Promotional allocations

Promotional SC may accompany qualifying GC purchases only when the operator and payment provider support the actual program. Server-side catalog terms control each allocation. Free SC requests are independent of purchase history.

A daily SC allowance is not promised by the current deployment. Any daily award, amount or interval must be defined in an active program. The current daily social-play reward is GC.

D

No-purchase entry methods

The active program exposes its available online free-request method, award, period and limits in the dashboard help panel. Eligible entrants sign in, accept the published rules, submit the free request and retain their immutable credit receipt.

Founder-proposed postal method

The supplied proposal requests a legibly handwritten white 3×5 card or unlined paper in a separately handwritten No. 10 envelope, sent by First-Class USPS mail. Required information: legal name, account email, connected Discord handle, residential return address and the following statement:

“I am requesting free promotional Sweeps Coins to participate in the skill-based simulation sweepstakes contests hosted by GridStake. I certify that I am at least 18 years of age, a resident of an authorized jurisdiction, and agree completely to the published Official Sweepstakes Rules.”

The proposal specifies 5.00 SC per verified physical request with no lifetime submission limit. Postal receipt processing is supported by a signed operator endpoint; it is not a self-service claim. Exact active postal terms must be published by the actual sponsor.

Do not send mail to the supplied “100 Production Way, Suite 400, Santa Monica, CA 90401” address. Ownership and mail processing at that address have not been verified. “GridStake Inc.” and “GridStake Aftermarket Logistics Inc.” were supplied sponsor names, not verified operating entities.

E

External races & atomic settlement

GridStake validates challenges held in external iRacing or ACC sessions. Both competitors accept immutable event, track, entry, currency and result terms before funding. GridStake is not the racing simulator.

Both entries are locked in the isolated ledger. The 10% rake is reserved; 90% forms the winner's prize. A verified fastest-clean-lap duel selects the lowest valid clean lap within the bound event window. Telemetry must identify the registered competitors and event; missing or invalid evidence cannot silently select a winner.

Ties, no clean laps and confirmed telemetry-deadline failures receive full, zero-fee refunds. A temporary provider or database interruption leaves the escrow held for retry. Refunds retain the original redeemable/non-redeemable SC classification.

F

Prize redemption & verification

Only SC won through validated competitive outcomes are redeemable. Promotional shop, free-entry and daily allocations do not become redeemable merely by being granted. The ledger separately tracks redeemable SC, preserves classification on refunds and prevents GC withdrawals.

The implemented policy is a minimum 50.00 SC, maximum 10,000.00 SC per rolling 24-hour period, and 1 eligible SC = $1 USD. Identity review, current program eligibility and a verified connected payout account are required. Documents and bank details are collected on the provider's hosted pages.

A request atomically reserves SC. The bank payout must be confirmed by the live provider before being recorded as paid. Failed bank payouts restore SC only after complete reversal is verified. Unknown provider outcomes remain held for reconciliation.

Actual paid and returned prizes have immutable tax records. Tax forms, thresholds and withholding depend on the applicable classification and current law. The supplied blanket “$600 net winnings means Form 1099-MISC” statement is not adopted. The operator's tax adviser must determine reporting and withholding for this program; users remain responsible for their own tax obligations.
